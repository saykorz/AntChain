  <h2>📌 What is AntChain?</h2>
  <p>
    <strong>AntChain</strong> is a decentralized timestamping and file-proof network, where every upload becomes part of a cryptographic chain. Built on the Autonomi network, it ensures that every file you submit is permanently recorded, time-stamped, and linked to the one before it — just like blocks in a blockchain, but powered by hardworking digital ants. 🐜
  </p>

  <h2>🎯 The Goal</h2>
  <p>
    To help people prove — with mathematical certainty — <strong>when</strong> they created or published something. Whether it's a song, a document, a design, or an idea, AntChain locks it in time and makes sure nobody can steal, fake, or rewrite your history.
  </p>

  <h2>💡 Description</h2>
  <p>
    AntChain addresses a simple but serious problem: <strong>how can you prove you made or said something first?</strong>
    <br /><br />
    Traditional archives and file uploads can be faked or erased. Blockchain systems can be expensive or limited. AntChain is lightweight, decentralized, and transparent:
  </p>
  <ul>
    <li>⏳ Every file upload is time-stamped on Autonomi</li>
    <li>🔗 It links to the previous file’s transaction, forming an unbroken chain</li>
    <li>💰 You pay a small amount to upload and secure your proof</li>
    <li>🧱 The more files come after yours, the harder it is to fake yours</li>
    <li>⚖️ This can serve as digital evidence in disputes, courts, or copyright claims</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>🚀 Features</h2>

  <h1>🧠 Can AntChain Be Faked?</h1>

  <p>
    Every system can theoretically be attacked — but not every system makes it practically possible. Here’s a breakdown of how someone might attempt to fake a record in AntChain and why it would almost certainly fail.
  </p>

  <h2>🧨 Theoretical Attack Scenario</h2>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Modify a previous file:</strong> Change the content or timestamp of a file that has already been uploaded</li>
    <li><strong>Rebuild the chain:</strong> Upload all following files again, creating a forged but consistent chain</li>
    <li><strong>Control the network:</strong> Hijack enough nodes to propagate the false version</li>
  </ul>
